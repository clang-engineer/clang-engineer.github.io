# Scene 3: Eight Mokjung Dance — 팔목중춤

## Characters

- **First through Eighth Mokjung (목중)** — eight masked monk characters
- **Jaebi (잽이)** — musicians who answer the performers' calls

## Costume and Performance Form

The Mokjung wear **deogeori (더거리)**, with blue and red upper-leg ties, yellow waistbands, white **gokkal**, and **hansam**. Each Mokjung enters separately, performs a characteristic entrance movement, exchanges a call with the musicians, gives a **bullim**, and dances. As successive Mokjung enter, they take positions around the edge of the performance ground facing the center.

## Performance Script

*The First Mokjung enters with his face covered by his left hansam and one hand open. He makes a broad circuit of the performance ground, comes to the center, turns first left and then right, swings the hansam upward, and leaps into the center. Looking right, he throws the right hansam; looking left, he throws the left. He scatters the right hansam and cries “Swi—,” then sits in the center with his face covered and speaks in a singing tone.*

**FIRST MOKJUNG:** Anaya!

**JAEBI:** Oi.

**FIRST MOKJUNG:** I live where broad fields spread out and farmland is plentiful. I set off to see the world. Here comes the *bullim*.

*Still seated, he continues in a singing tone.*

I saw Gyeongpodae in Gangneung, Naksansa in Yangyang, Mangyangjeong in Uljin, Jukseoru in Samcheok, Samilpo in Goseong, Chongseokjeong in Tongcheon, Wolsongjeong in Pyeonghae, and Cheongganjeong in Ganseong. After seeing all the famed sights, I reached Pyeongyang and found Yeongwangjeong magnificent.

The waters flowing from Deokcheon, Yangdeok, Maengsan, Samsu, and Gapsan wind their way around the blue-green cliffs by Bubyeongnu and become the Daedong River. Moranbong rises where the three mountains fall away, and Neungnado lies where the two waters divide.

I climbed Bubyeongnu, Yeongmyeongsa, and Eulmildae, visited the shrine of Gija, and went to the shrine of Guan Yu. There stood the lifelike image of Lord Guan, one of the three sworn brothers Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei. In a golden helmet and golden armor, his face was the color of a ripened jujube, his three-pointed beard bristling, phoenix eyes glaring, and his eighty-*geun* Green Dragon Blade raised before him. So stern was the sight that my head bowed of its own accord.

Leaving there, I passed Chilsongmun, stopped at Mansudae, and reached Botongmun. At Songbaekjeong, the sorrow of parting from one's beloved seemed to linger. Looking again toward the Daedong River, fishermen cast their lines into the water, while young women washed clothes along the bank. One maiden was especially splendid: her washing club rose and fell over her shoulder, and her beauty recalled the celebrated beauties Janggang and Xi Shi, with the figure of Lady Suk.

Looking and looking, my heart grew restless. I could stay no longer. I left it all behind, turned my steps toward Hwanghae Province, saw Yongyeon Falls at Guwolsan in Eunyul, and came down to Masup. Hearing the music ringing out, I thought: I too shall enjoy myself before I go.

**BULLIM:** *“Blue pine and green bamboo—the integrity of the gentleman!”*

*He dances to Taryeong jangdan.*

**FIRST MOKJUNG:** Swi—

*He retreats.*

**BULLIM:** *“Oak firewood, blazing firewood!”*

*He dances to Jajeun Domburi jangdan. As the First Mokjung reaches his final movement, the Second Mokjung enters with both hands open. The First takes his place near the edge of the performance ground, facing the center. The later Mokjung enter in the same general pattern and form a circle around the ground.*

---

*The Second Mokjung enters with both arms spread.*

**SECOND MOKJUNG:** Era, swii—. Anaya—

**JAEBI:** Oi.

**SECOND MOKJUNG:** Do all that lies within human power, and only then await Heaven's decree; honor the ancestral rites, and only then receive your guests. I have come here after circling Gaegol in the east, Guwolsan in the west, Jirisan in the south, and Hyangsan in the north, and then making my way through Gyeonggi Province. Fine performers are dancing here. Does a wild goose that has seen water fear the fisherman? Does a butterfly that has seen flowers fear a person? Does a bee that has tasted honey fear a beast? Play well and you are a *hallyang*; play poorly and you are only a passing guest. I too shall offer a *bullim*.

**BULLIM:** *“Under the clear blue sky and bright sun, purple clouds arise.”*

*He dances to Taryeong jangdan.*

---

*The Third Mokjung enters, shaking his outstretched arms up and down.*

**THIRD MOKJUNG:** Era, swi— Anaya—

**JAEBI:** Oi.

**THIRD MOKJUNG:** Here comes the *bullim*.

**BULLIM:** *“As Heaven adds to the years, may people add to their lives.”*

*He dances to Taryeong jangdan.*

---

*The Fourth Mokjung enters, swinging both arms forward and back.*

**FOURTH MOKJUNG:** Swii—. Anaya—

**JAEBI:** Oi.

**FOURTH MOKJUNG:** Here comes the *bullim*.

**BULLIM:** *“May the crane-haired elders of the household live a thousand years.”*

*He dances to Taryeong jangdan.*

---

*The Fifth Mokjung enters, sweeping his hansam right and left above his head.*

**FIFTH MOKJUNG:** Era, swii— Anaya—

**JAEBI:** Oi.

**FIFTH MOKJUNG:** Here comes the *bullim*.

**BULLIM:** *“May the descendants at one's knees enjoy glory for ten thousand generations.”*

*He dances to Taryeong jangdan.*

---

*The Sixth Mokjung enters with the same movement as the Third.*

**SIXTH MOKJUNG:** Era, swii— Anaya—

**JAEBI:** Oi.

**SIXTH MOKJUNG:** Here comes the *bullim*.

**BULLIM:** *“Wu and Chu spread away to the southeast—across the broad expanse.”*

*He dances to Taryeong jangdan.*

---

*The Seventh Mokjung enters with the same movement as the Second.*

**SEVENTH MOKJUNG:** Era, swii— Anaya—

**JAEBI:** Oi.

**SEVENTH MOKJUNG:** Here comes the *bullim*.

**BULLIM:** *“Among the flowers the butterflies dance, like swirling snow.”*

*He dances to Taryeong jangdan.*

---

*The Eighth Mokjung enters with the same movement as the First.*

**EIGHTH MOKJUNG:** Era, swii—. Anaya.

**JAEBI:** Oi.

**EIGHTH MOKJUNG:** Here comes the *bullim*.

**BULLIM:** *“Another heaven and earth—no realm of ordinary men.”*

*While the Eighth Mokjung dances, the First through Seventh Mokjung, already arranged in a circle, each continue their own dance.*

## Ensemble Dance — Mutdong-chum (뭇동춤)

*When the Eighth Mokjung finishes, all eight strike their hansam and cry “Swi—!” They then dance together to Jajeun Domburi jangdan. Forming a* **taegeuk-won (태극원)** *—a circular formation evoking the taegeuk—they dance out of the performance ground.*

**BULLIM:** *“Oak firewood, blazing firewood!”*

## Cultural and Translation Notes

### Deogeori (더거리)

The source explains *deogeori* as a three-paneled outer garment historically worn by certain military and official personnel in the Joseon period. The Korean name is retained because there is no precise modern English garment equivalent.

### Literary and Geographic Language

The First Mokjung's long travel speech incorporates famous scenic sites, classical Chinese allusions, and material resembling the western Korean *japga* song **Gyeongballim**. The translation therefore preserves proper names and the elevated catalogue-like movement of the speech rather than converting it into ordinary travel narration.

Several of the short *bullim* verses are quotations or adaptations of classical literary phrases. Their concise proverbial form is preserved in English while the Korean performance function remains marked as *bullim*.

### Hallyang (한량)

The Second Mokjung's expression contrasts someone who knows how to enjoy and perform properly with a mere passerby. *Hallyang* carries historical and cultural associations that exceed a single English equivalent, so it is provisionally retained here.
