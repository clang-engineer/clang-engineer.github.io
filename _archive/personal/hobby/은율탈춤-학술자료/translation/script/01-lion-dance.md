# Scene 1: Saja-chum (사자춤) — Lion Dance

**Source:** [8] 은율탈춤의 연희본 — Scene 1, p. 137 (PDF p. 3).

## Characters

- **Saja (사자)** — a large white lion operated by three performers

## Music

- **Taryeong jangdan (타령장단)**
- **Jajeun Domburi jangdan (잦은돔부리장단)**

## Performance Script

*After the* gilnori *ends, a large white lion, animated by three performers inside the costume, enters the performance ground. Accompanied by* Taryeong jangdan *and* Jajeun Domburi jangdan, *the lion dances vigorously and then exits.*

## Cultural and Performance Context

Its placement as the opening scene is significant. The source explicitly describes it as a **byeoksa (벽사)** ritual dance, intended to drive away harmful influences and purify the performance ground.

The Eunyul Talchum lion is also distinctive in construction. Whereas the lion figures in some other Korean mask-dance and lion-play traditions are operated by two performers, the Eunyul lion contains **three performers**.

For the broader meaning of *byeoksa*, see the project guide.
