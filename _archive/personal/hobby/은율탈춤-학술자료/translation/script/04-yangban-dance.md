# Scene 4: Yangban Dance — 양반춤

## Characters

- **Malttugi (말뚝이)** — servant and comic challenger of the Yangban
- **First, Second, and Third Yangban (양반 삼형제)** — three brothers representing the traditional elite
- **Saemaeksi (새맥시)** — young woman
- **Wonsungi (원숭이)** — monkey character
- **Choegwari (최괄이)** — comic male character corresponding broadly to the *Chwibari* type in related Hwanghae mask dramas

## Opening

*Malttugi enters holding a whip above his head with both hands and makes a circuit of the performance ground. He wears a* beonggeoji *hat, carries the whip in his right hand, and has a large bell fastened at his waist.*

**MALTTUGI:** Era, swii—. “Do all that lies within human power, and only then await Heaven's decree; honor the ancestral rites, and only then receive your guests.” So I too shall offer my greeting.

**BULLIM:** *“Blue pine and green bamboo—the integrity of the gentleman!”*

*Malttugi dances to Taryeong jangdan and exits.*

*The three Yangban brothers enter with Saemaeksi. The First and Second Yangban dance while moving their fans up and down. The Third performs a physically comic dance. The First wears a pale jade* dopo *and* yugeon; *the Second a* jeongjagwan; *the Third a* tanggeon. *Saemaeksi wears a green jacket with decorative trim, red skirt, dark-blue* kwaeja, *and* jokduri. *After circling the ground, they reach the center. Malttugi suddenly reappears.*

## Malttugi's Travel Speech

**MALTTUGI:** Era, swi—! I am Malttugi, the famous Malttugi of old, from long, long ago. I set out to see the Eight Provinces.

*He launches into an expansive catalogue of mountains, rivers, temples, fortresses, and celebrated places across Korea, moving from Gyeongsang and Jeolla through Chungcheong, Gyeonggi, Gangwon, Hamgyeong, Pyeongan, and finally Hwanghae Province. His speech incorporates phrases associated with pansori* danga *such as* Paldo Gangsan *and* Myeonggi Myeongchang.

**MALTTUGI:** ...At last I reached Guwolsan in Munhwa. From its summit I looked in every direction; Seoul's Namdaemun seemed to flicker before my eyes. Satgatbong stood beside me, Chodaebong was the blue dragon to the left, Jugaebong the white tiger to the right, and Meoksan in Eunyul and Wondusan in Pungcheon faced me across the land. I climbed Guwangsan, saw Guwang Cave, and came here. Seeing these bright-faced performers at play, I too shall enjoy a round before I go.

**BULLIM:** *“Kwaengchang, kwaengchang, kwae-gwaengchang—!”*

*Malttugi dances for some time to Domburi jangdan and exits.*

## The Yangban Call for Malttugi

**YANGBAN, TOGETHER:** Swi—!

**FIRST YANGBAN:** Brothers, listen to me.

**SECOND YANGBAN:** Yes, elder brother. Did you call?

**FIRST YANGBAN:** Listen carefully. Malttugi left us the year before last. I thought I heard the sound of his great bell here. Listen well; let us see whether we can find him.

**SECOND YANGBAN:** Yes, elder brother.

**FIRST YANGBAN:** Era, Malttugi!

*No answer.*

**FIRST YANGBAN:** Era, Malttugi!

*No answer.*

**FIRST YANGBAN:** Era, Malttugi—!

**MALTTUGI:** Yeees!

*He bounds in and lashes the First Yangban across the face with his whip.*

**FIRST YANGBAN:** *Recoiling, then stepping forward angrily.* You wretch! When a Yangban calls, you should bow politely and answer, “Did you call this humble servant?” What kind of manners are these?

**MALTTUGI:** *As though nothing happened, he rests the whip on his shoulder and strolls from side to side, then turns toward the First Yangban.* Yes, forgive me. It has been so long since I saw you that I was overcome with joy and confusion—and before I knew it, I had struck you over the head with this whip.

*He exits.*

---

**SECOND YANGBAN:** Era, Malttugi!

*No answer. He calls three times.*

**MALTTUGI:** Yeees!

*He bounds in, turns his backside toward the Second Yangban, and bends over. The Yangban leans down to inspect him. Just as his head reaches Malttugi's backside, Malttugi lets out an enormous fart.*

**SECOND YANGBAN:** Aigo! Elder brother! This fellow has fired a *daenggo*! I'm dying!

**FIRST YANGBAN:** What in the world happened?

**MALTTUGI:** *Laughing loudly.* Ha ha ha! I could never live as a Yangban. If you saw a real *daenggo*, you would surely faint!

*He exits.*

> **Note:** The source explains *daenggo* as “cannon,” used here as a comic metaphor for the explosive sound of the fart.

---

**THIRD YANGBAN:** Era, Malttugi—!

*After three calls, Malttugi answers, rushes in, and sits down on top of the Third Yangban.*

**THIRD YANGBAN:** Brothers! Malttugi is sitting on me! Come and stop him!

**FIRST & SECOND YANGBAN:** What is happening here?

**MALTTUGI:** I was protecting our lord's legs in case he should fall.

**THIRD YANGBAN:** *Laughing strangely.* Ah, of course. That's our Malttugi. Malttugi—

**MALTTUGI:** Yes—

**THIRD YANGBAN:** Now the young lady will call you. Conduct yourself properly. She has been feeling lonely of late, so answer politely, ask after her from head to toe, and perhaps even give her a kiss. And since your nose is so large, I imagine your balls must be large as well.

**MALTTUGI:** *Mockingly.* It grieves me that my lord has become such a cripple.

## Saemaeksi, Malttugi, and the Monkey

**SAEMAEKSI:** Era, Malttugi—

*No answer. She calls three times.*

**MALTTUGI:** Yeees!

*He bounds over, takes both of Saemaeksi's wrists, and fondles them. She turns her face away and lowers her head in apparent embarrassment.*

**MALTTUGI:** Ah! The young lady is pleased—yin and yang begin to turn, all three hundred and sixty-four vital points in my body tremble, and the thing hanging below has taken notice and is nodding away. What am I to do?

*At that moment the Monkey somersaults and rolls into the scene. It slaps Saemaeksi's hands free from Malttugi's grasp and scratches at him.*

**MALTTUGI:** Aigo!

*Startled, he retreats awkwardly and exits.*

*The Monkey circles Saemaeksi and dances. Saemaeksi joins it. The Monkey performs an overtly sexual comic dance, at times climbing onto her back. After the Monkey exits, Saemaeksi develops labor pains and gives birth in the center of the performance ground.*

**JAEBI:** Oi! She's had a baby!

## Choegwari and the Child

*Choegwari rushes in.*

**CHOEGWARI:** Hey, that's my child!

*He takes the baby.*

Ah, what a handsome little fellow! Like father, like son, they say—and look at him! I've lived all these years without a son, and this is the first child I've ever had. Let me hold him and play with him.

*He dandles the child.*

Eoreureung kkakkung! Eoreureung kkakkung! Hey, you—stand up straight!

*He balances the child on his hand.*

Kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk— aigo! Kkoduk— kkoduk— kkoduk—!

*He repeats the play several times.*

That's it! Now let us sing the **Kkodugi Taryeong (꼬둑이타령)**.

### Kkodugi Taryeong

**CHOEGWARI:**  
Kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk—Kkodugi, my child!  
Could silver buy you? Could even gold buy you?  
Kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk—Kkodugi, my child!

Did you drop straight down from Heaven,  
or spring suddenly up from the earth?  
Kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk—Kkodugi, my child!

May you be a loyal child to the nation,  
a filial child to your parents,  
a child of harmony among your kin,  
of brotherly love among siblings,  
of trust among friends,  
and the darling of every neighborhood.  
Are you a child of gold, a child of silver?  
Among ten thousand folds of green mountains, you are a child of treasure.

*The song continues through a long chain of wordplay, tree names, classical imagery, and a comic game of Korean chess in which historical heroes are assigned to the pieces.*

**CHOEGWARI:** Kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk, kkoduk—Kkodugi, my child! Oi—look at him smiling away!

*Domburi jangdan begins. Choegwari continues to dandle the child, then dances to the rhythm and exits together with Saemaeksi.*

## Translation Notes

The extended travel catalogue and the latter portion of **Kkodugi Taryeong** contain dense geographical, literary, and verbal wordplay. They are preserved in the source-based working translation without replacing Korean place names or historical references with generalized English equivalents.

The sexual comedy in this scene is explicit in the source and is translated directly rather than euphemized, because it is part of the scene's comic and satirical performance structure.
